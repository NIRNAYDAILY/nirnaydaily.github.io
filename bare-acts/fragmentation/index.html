15. Government may of its own accord or on application declare its intention to make

scheme for consolidation of holdings.— With the object of consolidating holdings in any village, mahal, 5[taluka or tahsil] or any part thereof for the purpose of better cultivation of lands therein, the 6 [State] Government may 7[of its own motion or on an application made in that behalf declare] by a notification in the Official Gazette and by publication in the prescribed manner in the village or villages concerned its intention to make a scheme for the consolidation of holdings in such village or

villages or part thereof as may be specified. On such publication in the village concerned the 8[State]
