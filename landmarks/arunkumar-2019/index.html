Arunkumar & Anr. v. The Inspector General of Registration & Ors.

W.P. (MD) No. 4125 of 2019
Madras High Court · Single judge · Decided 22 April 2019

Held: A marriage between a Hindu man and a Hindu transwoman is valid under Section 5 of the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955, and must be registered. (G.R. Swaminathan, J.)
