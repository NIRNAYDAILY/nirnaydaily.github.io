Naz Foundation v. Government of NCT of Delhi & Ors.

160 (2009) DLT 277W.P. (C) No. 7455 of 2001
Delhi High Court · 2-member bench · Decided 2 July 2009

Held: Read down Section 377 IPC to decriminalise consensual sexual acts between adults in private.

Reversed by the Supreme Court in Suresh Kumar Koushal (2013). The position was restored in Navtej Singh Johar (2018).
