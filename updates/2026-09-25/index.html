Supreme Court · Decided 24 Sep 2026 · POCSO / Family

'R' v. 'E' & Anr. (and connected matters)

2026 LiveLaw (SC) 982 · 2026 INSC 1049 · C.A. No. 13119/2026
Coram: J.B. Pardiwala & K.V. Viswanathan, JJ.

Safeguards against misuse of POCSO in custody fights

HeldWhere a parent is accused under POCSO during a matrimonial dispute, there is no automatic arrest on an FIR and a child psychiatrist or psychologist must be consulted first. The Court quashed a mother's FIR against the father, which the CBI found unsupported, and asked the Family Court to oversee the child's return to him.

Why it matters: Police and courts now have national guardrails for POCSO complaints made in custody battles.
