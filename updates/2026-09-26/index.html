Supreme Court · Decided 25 Sep 2026 · Criminal / Evidence

Paramjit Singh Bedi v. State of Punjab

2026 LiveLaw (SC) 992
Coram: Ujjal Bhuyan & Atul S. Chandurkar, JJ.

Rape conviction set aside: testimony can't be split

HeldThe Court acquitted the appellant, holding that a court cannot accept part of the prosecutrix's statement and reject the rest. Material gaps in her account meant the conviction could not rest on her word alone.

Why it matters: A conviction on the victim's sole testimony still needs an account that is reliable as a whole.
