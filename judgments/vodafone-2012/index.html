Vodafone International Holdings B.V. v. Union of India & Anr.

(2012) 6 SCC 613
Decided 20 January 2012 · 3-judge bench · 274 pages · Official text of the Supreme Court of India

Held: The offshore transfer of shares between two non-resident companies was not taxable in India, so Vodafone was not liable to deduct tax at source.

Parliament then amended the Income Tax Act retrospectively in 2012, a change reversed in 2021.

Section 195 casts an obligation on the payer to deduct
