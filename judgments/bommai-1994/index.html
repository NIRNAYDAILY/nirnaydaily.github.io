S.R. Bommai v. Union of India

(1994) 3 SCC 1AIR 1994 SC 1918
Decided 11 March 1994 · 9-judge bench · 357 pages · Official text of the Supreme Court of India

Held: President's Rule under Article 356 is subject to judicial review. A government's majority must be tested on the floor of the House, and secularism is part of the basic structure.
